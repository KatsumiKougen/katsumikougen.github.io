Neil Buchanan

I've been into drawing since I was a school kid.

Now as a college student, my skills are improving more and more.

You might be wondering why I don't share my work on platforms like Purgatory DeviantArt or Pixiv.

The thing is, DeviantArt has a questionable reputation, so it's not really my scene. As for other platforms, I'm not a dedicated professional artist and don't want to put myself alongside the experts.

I simply draw because I enjoy it. I'm competent but don't see myself as an expert. Drawing is just a pastime for me, however you interpret fun in art. I'm no Da Vinci; I'm just a guy who's good at a few things, drawing included. And I don't do it very often—I mostly spend time browsing online or tinkering with Python and JS coding when bored. So no commissions, thanks.

Kit from "Gameoverse" in the Style of Derrick J. Wyatt

I drew this exactly one day after the premiere of Glitch Productions' latest animated show "Gameoverse". This is a show about a catgirl named Kit Bodega who travels to different video game worlds within the titular Gameoverse to make sure the villains of each world stays undefeated; otherwise that game world will be destroyed. Yeah, TL;DR it's "Wreck-It Ralph" meets inverted "Penn Zero".

The plot boils down to "the hero working for someone else goes to places to uphold the status quo" — which has been done before, but it's not a bad thing; the characters are OK, the soundtrack is nice, and the animation is perfect — the art style reminds me of Derrick J. Wyatt...

which is exactly why I drew this sh*t.

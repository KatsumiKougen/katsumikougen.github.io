Collection of "Three Kingdoms" Jokes

I can't believe I had to lurk every single Vietnamese message board for this sh*t. But here we go.

This is a compilation of "Three Kingdoms" humour from Vietnam, where the material is known as Truyện cười Tam Quốc (Three Kingdoms Jokes) or Tam Quốc chí xuyên tạc (Three Kingdoms: Twisted Tales). These stories have been making the rounds on Vietnamese forums since at least the 2000s, taking the well-known feats of Liu Bei, Cao Cao, Sun Quan, Zhuge Liang, and their contemporaries and give them a decidedly undignified comedic makeover.

While the original novel is packed with court intrigue, military manoeuvring, bitter rivalries, and outsized characters, faithfulness to the Three Kingdoms period is decidedly not a priority for these stories. Instead, its famous warlords and strategists are thrown into silly mix-ups, anachronistic predicaments, lowbrow comedy, and punchlines that would stick out badly in a genuine historical epic. A handful of entries hold onto recognisable features of the original storyline, while the rest just borrow the cast and their names for jokes that might easily have been told about people today. (And yes, some of these may ring a bell if you've read my collection of Vietnamese jokes.)

Fun fact: the Vietnamese word xuyên tạc — to distort or misrepresent — fits this comedic tradition remarkably well. Rather than correcting the historical record, these stories gleefully take a sledgehammer to it for laughs. A brilliant strategist may be undone by something as simple as a foolish mix-up, while a fearsome warlord's greatest preoccupation may turn out to be remarkably banal. Time and again, the humour springs from the gap between the characters' mythic reputations and the goofy situations they end up in.

Throughout the 2000s and early 2010s, countless compilations sprang up across Vietnamese forums and blogs, where users pulled stories from all over, contributed personal variations, and at times recycled content that had long been circulating elsewhere.

These jokes are a prime example of Vietnamese internet humour's more defiantly disrespectful side, in which one of the most revered epics in Chinese literature serves as a bottomless well of ever more undignified amusement.

And now the whole set has been translated into English for your enjoyment (or your gallows humour, if your mind isn't the sharpest tool). This one goes out to "Dynasty Warriors" fans, "Fate/Grand Order" (and any "Nasuverse") fans, and anyone who's read the original novel. Have a good time, and remember to leave your brains at the entrance.

Warning: a number of these jokes feature sexual references, lowbrow anatomical comedy, adultery storylines, dated jokes about homosexuality, and whatnot. Others involve sexualised humiliation, foul language, and punchlines that turn violent. It wouldn't be fair to call the whole page NSFW, but it's decidedly not PG-13 either. Reader's discretion is advised. (And please, if your children have this page up, get them to close the tab.)

Liu Bei: "Yun, go to the back kitchen and bring out the pot of porridge!"

Zhao Yun saluted and scurried off, returning a moment later with a massive pot. Everyone dug in while drinking. But soon...

Liu Bei: "Yun, where did you get this porridge? Why is it so chewy and rough?"

Guan Yu: "Yeah, what kind of porridge is this? It's just bone fragments!"

Zhang Fei: "And what kind of rice clumps together into solid blocks like this?!"

[Silence]

Lady Gan (off-screen): "Oh lord in heaven! Who stole my pig feed?!"

The Roach Phobia

Guan Yu was a god-like warrior on the battlefield, but at home, he was an absolute puppy in front of his wife. He charged into enemy ranks like a wolf among sheep, yet his wife was a literal lioness. Moreover, Guan Yu had a crippling phobia of cockroaches. Why? Because Guan Yu was terrified of his wife, and his wife was terrified of roaches!

Wife: "You're so good at fighting wars, but how come every time you clean the house I still find cockroaches?! From now on, if I see even one roach, you can say bye-bye to one of your teeth, understand?"

Naturally, Guan Yu had no choice but to beg Hua Tuo for help: "You went to vocational veterinary school, right? Please give me some roach killer or my wife is going to murder me."

Hua Tuo: "Here, take this bottle. Spray it around the house and I guarantee every single roach in your place will pack up and march over to wage war on Zhang Fei's roaches instead. Just read the instructions carefully."

A few days later, Hua Tuo dropped by to check on Guan Yu and found a tall, long-bearded, toothless old man eating porridge.

Hua Tuo: "Excuse me, sir, can I please see Guan Yu?"

Old Man: "I am Guan Yu, who else would it be?!"

Hua Tuo: "Good lord, where did all your teeth go?! The long beard plus the sunken toothless mouth made me think you were some random grandpa..."

Guan Yu: "Damn it! Thanks to your medicine, all I can eat now is porridge..."

Hua Tuo: "What? It's great medicine!"

Guan Yu: "Yeah, too great... the roaches from my house marched over to Zhang Fei's place, fought ferociously, and dragged back countless prisoners of war..."

The Fruit Basket Trap

One day, the sworn brothers Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei snuck over to East Wu for some undercover scouting, taking the opportunity to grab a shampoo and massage. But since brothers share everything, each of them assumed someone else was paying, meaning absolutely none of them brought any cash. After the bouncers kindly upgraded them with a complimentary teeth-and-gums smoothie, the three brothers were tied up and dragged before Sun Quan.

Sun Quan: "Audacious! The crime of espionage can be forgiven, but getting a massage without tipping the staff is a death sentence. Since we're somewhat old acquaintances, I'll torture you guys a bit before I execute you. Do this for me: each of you go find 10 pieces of any fruit and bring them back here. Put a jet engine on your ass and hurry up!"

In East Wu, finding a chatroom or karaoke bar was easy, but finding a fruit stall was shockingly hard. Still, clinging to life, the three brothers busted their humps. By evening, Liu Bei returned with 10 oranges, Guan Yu brought back 10 grapes, and Zhang Fei's phone went straight to voicemail.

Sun Quan: "Good. Now for task number two: shove every single piece of fruit you collected up your ass. The first one to make a single peep gets executed. Whoever pulls it off goes free."

Liu Bei gritted his teeth, bulging his eyes, and managed to squeeze in one orange, but when he tried the second, the agony was too much and he let out an Ouch!

Sun Quan: "Throw him in the dungeon, execution at dawn!"

Tearful, Liu Bei turned to Guan Yu: "Guess my luck just ran out, man. Die I must. With those tiny wild grapes of yours, you'll definitely survive. Farewell to you and Fei... make sure you avenge me!"

Just a little while later, however, Liu Bei was shocked to see Guan Yu tossed into the dungeon right beside him to await execution.

Liu Bei: "What the hell?! My oranges were huge, so my death makes sense... but how the hell did you die with tiny grapes?!"

Guan Yu: "Damn it all... I was cruising smoothly through the first nine grapes... and right on the tenth one, I burst out laughing and died."

Liu Bei: "Why the hell would you laugh, you dumbass?!"

Guan Yu: "Because right at that exact second, I looked over and saw Zhang Fei dragging 10 whole durians across the finish line..."

The Funeral Procession

One day, Zhang Fei was out on patrol when he ran into Guan Yu standing there with dark eye bags, muttering to himself.

Zhang Fei: "Hey, what's wrong with your eyes? Did your wife beat you up again? Your wife is a total MMA fighter."

Guan Yu: "No! I just couldn't sleep."

Zhang Fei: "Man, I was about to ask what you dreamed about last night so I could play the lottery... so why couldn't you sleep?"

Guan Yu: "Well... there was a dead flea on my bed."

Zhang Fei: "So what? It's dead, how could it bite you to keep you awake?"

Guan Yu: "Yeah, but because it died, its entire extended family came out to hold a massive funeral procession for it, and the noise drove me insane."

The Under-the-Bed Deduction

One day, Liu Bei, Zhuge Liang, and Pang Tong were sitting around looking utterly miserable, commiserating with one another.

Liu Bei: "Man, I'm so depressed. My wife is having an affair with Zhao Yun."

Zhuge Liang & Pang Tong: "Wait, My Lord, how on earth do you know that?!"

Liu Bei: "Because I came home yesterday and found a gorgeous dragon spear under her bed..."

Zhuge Liang sighed: "Sigh... in that case, my wife must be fooling around with that tiger-bearded, snake-spear-wielding freak Zhang Fei..."

Liu Bei & Pang Tong: "Whoa, how do you know?!"

Zhuge Liang: "Because I found a massive Serpent Halberd under my wife's bed."

Pang Tong panicked: "Then I am 100% sure my wife is cheating on me with Red Hare the warhorse!"

Liu Bei & Zhuge Liang: "Oh my god! How could you possibly know that?!"

Pang Tong: "Because when I walked into the bedroom just now, I found Guan Yu hiding under the bed!"

The Snowball of Bad News

Lü Bu was an invincible war god who struck terror into enemies wherever he went. Off the battlefield, however, Lü Bu was an animal lover, a ladies' man, a devoted son—basically, he loved everything.

Before heading out to campaign on the remote frontier, Lü Bu instructed Li Ru: "Listen, normally you just gamble and play illegal lottery at home... while I'm away fighting, take good care of my family and livestock, got it? Report back to me once a month with gold prices, dollar exchange rates, and home updates."

One month later, Li Ru arrived at the military camp to meet Lü Bu.

Li Ru: "I'm so sorry, boss... but your favorite pet dog passed away."

Lü Bu: "What?! I specifically told you to take great care of him!"

Li Ru: "Well, Physician Hua Tuo said he died of severe indigestion from overeating..."

Lü Bu: "Huh?! I told you to feed him a balanced, scientific diet!"

Li Ru: "Yes, boss, but after Red Hare died, the dog kept wandering over to eat out of the horse trough..."

Lü Bu: "WHAT THE HELL?! What did you just say?! My glorious Red Hare is DEAD?!"

Li Ru: "Yes... the stable caught fire, so..."

Lü Bu: "WHAT?! How the hell did the stable catch fire, you idiot?!"

Li Ru: "Ah, well, your castle caught fire first, and the flames spread to the stable..."

Lü Bu: "OH NO! What did you say?! MY CASTLE BURNED DOWN?!"

Li Ru: "Yes, because the candles around your mother's coffin caught the window curtains on fire..."

Lü Bu: "Good god... COFFIN?! MY MOTHER?! Say that again, what happened to my mother?!"

Li Ru: "She died of a massive heart attack, sir. She went into cardiac arrest when she found out your wife Diaochan ran away with Zhang Liao..."

The Cheat Sheet Failure

Zhang Liao and Lü Bu were captured and bound by Cao Cao. Cao Cao ordered all prisoners executed, but since he respected true martial talent, he considered recruiting Lü Bu and Zhang Liao. Cao Cao ordered Xun Yu to test them to see if they were worthy recruits—or if they should just be executed to join their buddies.

Lü Bu was overjoyed, but whispered to Zhang Liao: "Man, I only know how to kill people and steal women! I have no idea how multiple-choice tests work. You go in first, do your best, and throw me some answers!"

Wanting to repay Lü Bu's loyalty, a trembling Zhang Liao walked into the interrogation room where Xun Yu, Cao Cao, and an executioner holding a gleaming machete were waiting.

Xun Yu: "I will ask only three questions. If Cao Cao approves of your answers, you join our army."

Zhang Liao: "Y-yes... go ahead, sir."

Xun Yu: "What do you do if you encounter Lord Cao Cao's enemy?"

Zhang Liao: "I will chop him to pieces immediately!"

(Xun Yu and Cao Cao nodded in approval)

Xun Yu: "What do you do if you meet the Han Emperor?"

Zhang Liao: "I will joyfully wave and greet him!"

Xun Yu: "What if Lord Cao Cao orders you into battle?"

Zhang Liao: "I will say: 'Yes, My Lord!'"

Thrilled with the answers, Cao Cao ordered Zhang Liao untied and recruited.

Zhang Liao rushed out and quickly gave Lü Bu the cheat sheet: "Just remember these three exact answers in order:

  1. I will chop him to pieces immediately!
  2. I will joyfully wave and greet him!
  3. Yes, My Lord!"

Lü Bu mumbled the answers to memorise them, then walked inside shaking.

Xun Yu: "What do you do if you meet the Han Emperor?"

Lü Bu: "I will chop him to pieces immediately!"

Cao Cao turned pale green with rage. Xun Yu quickly asked the second question:

Xun Yu: "What do you do if you encounter Lord Cao Cao's enemy?"

Lü Bu: "I will joyfully wave and greet him!"

At this point, Cao Cao couldn't take it anymore. He slammed the table and roared: "Are you screwing with me, you son of a bitch?! DO YOU WANT TO DIE?!"

Lü Bu: "Yes, My Lord!"

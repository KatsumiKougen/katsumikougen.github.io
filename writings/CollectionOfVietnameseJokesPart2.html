The Ubiquitous Bay

A foreign tourist asked his tour guide:

"I've been to Vietnam many times. I've already visited two famous bays: Nha Trang Bay and Ha Long Bay. But there's one other bay I see advertised everywhere. Can you take me there?"

The guide asked curiously, "Could you tell me the name of that bay?"

The tourist pointed at a sign on a street wall and stuttered out the letters: "C-A-M-D-A-I-B-A-Y".

(Note: "Cấm đái bậy" means "No public urination".)

The Technical Talk

A little boy asked his dad, "Daddy, where do babies come from?"

His father, an IT engineer who was browsing the web at the time, casually replied:

"Well, your mother and I were browsing the web together on a bed. I connected to your mother. I uploaded data from a USB drive into her system. Once the download finished, your mother was shocked to discover she hadn't installed any anti-virus software, and I hadn't set up a firewall."

"What happened next, Daddy?"

"Both of us tried to delete the data and even format the drive, but it was too late. And that, after a few months, is how you were born."

Red Tape Blessing

A poor woman stepped inside a church to pray. Weeping, she prayed to God: "Oh Lord, my family is struggling so much. Please grant me 200 thousand dongs so I can buy some rice for my kids."

As she was praying, a local government official happened to walk past. Hearing her plea, he pulled out a 100-thousand-dong note from his pocket, placed it next to her, and left a note saying: "This money is provided by the Party and the State, not by God."

When the woman opened her eyes and saw the money, instead of being overjoyed, she burst into an even louder cry: "Oh Lord! Next time You send me money, please hand it to me directly—don't route it through the Party and the State!"

Volatile Assets

During a high school chemistry practical session, the teacher was explaining the various physical properties of gold.

"Aside from gold's obvious characteristics—such as its shiny appearance, high thermal conductivity, and excellent electrical conductivity—can anyone tell me another key property of gold?"

Noticing a student nodding off in the back, the teacher called him up to the front. "You there, tell the class: what is another property of gold?"

The kid rubbed his eyes and replied, "Sir, gold is extremely volatile—it evaporates instantly."

"Are you sure about that?" the teacher asked, raising an eyebrow.

"Absolutely, sir! If you don't believe me, just leave a gold bar out on the sidewalk and see how fast it vanishes!"

Lost in Translation, Part 4

In the middle of a screaming match between his parents, a little boy ran up to his dad and asked, "Dad, what's my name?"

Furious, his dad snapped, "You bitch!"

The little boy turned to his mom and asked, "Mom, what's my name?"

Irritated, his mom yelled, "You asshole!"

Confused, the boy went to his older sister, who was blasting pop music in her room. "Sis, what's my name?" She just sang along to her headphones: "Oh! Yeah!"

Finally, he went to his older brother, who was intensely watching a soccer match on TV. "Bro, what's my name?" His brother shouted at the screen: "David Beckham!"

The next day at school, the teacher asked him, "What is your name, young man?"

"You bitch!" the boy answered proudly.

Shocked, the teacher gasped, "Excuse me?! What did you just say?"

"You asshole!"

Red-faced, the teacher shouted, "I am expelling you from school!"

"Oh! Yeah!" the boy cheered.

"Who on earth do you think you are?!" the teacher demanded.

"David Beckham!"

Holding On for Dear Life

Seth Lowell and Jane Doe were asleep in bed when Jane suddenly bolted upright, gasping for air.

Seth immediately sat up to comfort her. "Honey, calm down! Take a deep breath. What happened?"

With a pale face, sweat dripping down her forehead, and a trembling voice, Jane whimpered, "I had a terrible nightmare!"

"Is that so?" Seth listened intently, trying to be supportive. "What did you dream about?"

"I dreamt I was falling off a massive cliff into a dark abyss!" Jane explained. "Luckily, right before I hit the bottom, I managed to grab hold of a thick, sturdy tree trunk with roots sprawling everywhere. That's how I survived!"

Seeing that his wife had finally calmed down, Seth sighed loudly. "Are you fully awake now?"

"Yes... I'm awake," Jane gasped.

"Great. Then let go of the 'tree trunk' so I can get some sleep!" Seth snapped, thoroughly exhausted after enduring her grip all night.

Extreme Camouflage

"Why on earth did you blow our unit's concealed ambush position during the field exercises last night? Not only that, but you hurled insults at two local civilians and nearly assaulted them!"

The Department Store for Husbands

At a 6-story "Husband Store", women could browse and purchase a husband. Right at the entrance hung a sign detailing the rules:

  • The higher you go, the better the quality of the men.
  • You may only choose as you ascend floor by floor; going back down to re-select is strictly forbidden.

A woman stopped at the sign, read it, and decided to step inside to try her luck.

On the entrance to Floor 1, a sign read: "Men who have steady jobs."

"Not bad," she thought, but she immediately proceeded to the next level.

The sign on Floor 2 read: "Men who have steady jobs and love children."

She kept climbing.

The sign on Floor 3 read: "Men who have steady jobs, love children, and are extremely handsome."

"Now we're talking!" she exclaimed, but her feet carried her up to Floor 4.

The sign on Floor 4 read: "Men who have steady jobs, love children, are extremely handsome, and help with housework."

"Amazing!" she gasped—yet she pushed on to Floor 5.

The sign on Floor 5 read: "Men who have steady jobs, love children, are extremely handsome, help with housework, and are deeply romantic."

She was sorely tempted to stop here, but driven by curiosity, she gathered her resolve and climbed up to Floor 6.

On Floor 6, she found a giant sign that read:

"You are visitor #1,108,159 to this floor. There are no men on this floor. It exists solely to prove that it is impossible to satisfy a woman. Thank you for visiting!"

Flawless Experimental Logic

A zoologist was conducting a scientific study on grasshoppers.

He placed a healthy grasshopper on a table, stared at it intently, slammed his hand on the desk, and shouted: "JUMP!"

The grasshopper immediately leaped across the table. The scientist meticulously noted this in his journal.

Next, he carefully snipped off the grasshopper's two large hind legs, placed it back on the table, slammed his hand down, and shouted: "JUMP!"

The grasshopper remained completely motionless.

The scientist nodded thoughtfully, opened his journal, and wrote down his final scientific conclusion:

"When you cut off a grasshopper's two hind legs, it becomes completely deaf."

Proportional Imperial Features

One day, Emperor Wu of Han turned to his court ministers and remarked:

"I read in a book of physiognomy that if a person's philtrum—the groove above the upper lip—is one inch long, they will live to be a hundred years old."

Standing nearby, the court jester Dongfang Shuo burst into uncontrollable laughter.

The ministers immediately scolded him for his insolence toward the Emperor.

Dongfang Shuo took off his hat and bowed low. "Forgive me, Your Imperial Majesty! I would never dare laugh at Your Highness—I was merely laughing at poor old Peng Zu's face!"

The Emperor asked, "And why would you laugh at Peng Zu?"

Dongfang Shuo replied, "Legend has it that Peng Zu lived for eight hundred years. If what Your Majesty read in the physiognomy book is true, Peng Zu's philtrum must have been eight inches long! And if his philtrum was eight inches long, his entire face must have been at least ten feet tall!"

Emperor Wu burst out laughing and pardoned his jester on the spot.

Reverse Engineering

A high school teacher was lecturing on technological progress:

"Class, in the near future, advanced machinery will completely replace human labour. A single machine will do the work of five to seven factory workers. For instance, engineers will build a machine where you insert a whole cow into one end, and it outputs delicious sausages from the other!"

A hesitant student raised his hand. "Sir... if people get tired of eating sausages, can you put a sausage back into the machine and get a whole cow out?"

The teacher paused and looked at him. "How old are you, son?"

"Eighteen, sir," the student replied.

The teacher smiled gently. "The machine you're describing has existed for a very long time. Nineteen years ago, a man inserted a single sausage into a machine, and nine months later, it produced a cow!"
